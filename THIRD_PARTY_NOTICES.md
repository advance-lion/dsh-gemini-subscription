This plugin uses the host's MIT-licensed DeepSeek Harness LLM interfaces.
The separate local bridge installs @rynfar/meridian 1.93.0 (MIT), retaining
its package, notices and dependency licenses. Source: https://github.com/rynfar/meridian.

to 1.5.10. Windows acceptance is enabled through the library's public option.
No other upstream validation, account check or permission hook is removed.
